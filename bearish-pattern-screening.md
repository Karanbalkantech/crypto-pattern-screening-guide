# How to screen crypto for bearish chart patterns

How to screen crypto for bearish chart patterns starts with finding candidate charts, identifying weakening price structure, and checking whether the evidence supports your interpretation. ChartScout can support the discovery stage by bringing detected crypto chart patterns into one view through its Pattern Screener.

The important distinction is that discovering a pattern is not the same as confirming a breakdown or deciding to trade. A useful screening process keeps those decisions separate.

This guide explains how to build a bearish-pattern research list without treating every weak-looking chart as a prediction.

## 1. Define what you want to investigate

Before browsing results, choose your research timeframe and the markets you want to examine.

Also decide whether you are studying a possible reversal after an advance or a continuation inside an existing decline.

Those are different research questions. A small bearish formation inside a larger uptrend may require a different interpretation from a similar formation within a sustained downtrend.

Write down your criteria before examining candidates. Avoid changing them simply because another chart looks more convincing.

## 3. Identify the structure before trusting the label

A pattern name should help you describe a chart, not replace your examination of it.

### Bear flag

A bear flag generally consists of a sharp decline followed by a smaller consolidation that moves sideways or slopes upward.

The initial decline forms the flagpole. The consolidation forms the flag.

When reviewing the structure, identify the consolidation boundaries and assess whether price remains inside them or has moved below the lower boundary.

### Descending triangle

A descending triangle generally combines a relatively horizontal support area with a sequence of lower highs.

The falling highs suggest that rallies are reaching lower levels, while repeated lows identify an area where buying has previously appeared.

The pattern remains unresolved while price stays within its boundaries. A move below support needs further assessment rather than an automatic bearish conclusion.

### Rising wedge

A rising wedge has two upward-sloping boundaries that converge.

It may appear during an advance or within a recovery during a broader decline. Its interpretation depends on that surrounding context.

Identify both boundaries clearly. If the shape only appears after repeatedly adjusting the lines, the candidate may be too ambiguous for your research rules.

## 4. Record the current stage

Give every candidate a factual status:

- Forming within its boundaries.
- Testing an important boundary.
- Closed beyond the boundary on the review timeframe.
- Returned inside the formation.
- No longer relevant to the original interpretation.

These labels prevent a developing pattern from being described as an established breakdown.

A wick below support and a candle close below support are different observations. Record what happened without adding certainty that the chart does not provide.

## 5. Look for reasons the bearish interpretation could fail

Examine nearby support, the larger-timeframe structure, and recent volume on the market you are reviewing.

A potential decline may encounter another support area shortly below the pattern. A smaller bearish formation may also conflict with a stronger structure on a larger timeframe.

Compare volume within the same exchange and instrument. Do not assume that one volume observation represents activity across the entire crypto market.

Write down contradicting evidence alongside supporting evidence. If the boundaries are unclear or the surrounding chart conflicts with your requirements, rejecting the candidate is a valid result.

## 7. Keep a screening journal

For each retained chart, record:

- Review date and timezone.
- Market and exchange.
- Timeframe.
- Observed formation.
- Current pattern stage.
- Relevant boundary.
- Nearby support.
- Supporting observations.
- Contradicting observations.
- What would invalidate the interpretation.
- Decision: reject, monitor, or investigate further.

When your interpretation changes, add a dated update rather than rewriting the original observation.

## What a useful screening session produces

The result should be a manageable list of charts with clear reasons for inclusion.

It does not need to produce a trade. A session that rejects unsuitable candidates can still fulfil its purpose.
